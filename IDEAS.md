## From the B playtest (2026-10-20)
- Balatro as the reference for mode B: set up a play, see a preview of a big outcome, then watch it pay off (anticipation, then satisfaction). Brian's note.
- B's preview is worth more with richer mechanics than the basic combo: anything whose outcome depends on order (scaling, triggers, multipliers) gains from an exact preview.
- B with draw: today a draw card resolves and locks when queued; a hybrid (plan in segments, each draw opens a new segment) could keep card draw in B.

## From the C playtest (2026-10-20)
- C's open design problem (Brian): with no mana cost, cards lose a key power differentiator; a roguelike needs tradeoffs. Directions to explore: a per-card "weight" that eats the 5-card limit or the timer (heavy cards count as 2, or shorten the next pick window), cards that only pay in a certain combo slot (opener / middle / closer), spirit-number tradeoffs (strong cards with awkward numbers), self-costs (HP, discard).
